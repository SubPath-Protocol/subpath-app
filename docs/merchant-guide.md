# Merchant Guide

This guide explains how to create and manage subscription plans on SubPath.

## Creating a Plan

1. Connect your Stellar wallet to the SubPath dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Create Plan**.
3. Specify the **Token Address** (e.g., the Native XLM SAC or a custom stablecoin).
4. Enter the **Amount** to charge per cycle.
5. Set the **Cycle Duration** in seconds (e.g., `2592000` for 30 days).
6. Sign and submit the transaction.
7. Note your resulting **Plan ID**.

## Viewing Subscribers

The SubPath Indexer automatically tracks your plan's subscribers. In the dashboard, you can view all active, paused, and canceled subscriptions associated with your Plan ID, along with their next eligible billing timestamp.
